# BLIKK

## Vision

BLIKK is not trying to recreate GunZ.

BLIKK is building the next evolution of high skill movement combat.

Movement comes before damage.

Expression comes before statistics.

Players should constantly feel themselves improving.

Every mechanic should be easy to understand but extremely difficult to master.

The goal is for players to say:

"I can always get better."

---

## Design Philosophy

Movement is the game.

Weapons enhance movement.

Combat rewards mastery.

Animations communicate gameplay.

Responsiveness is always more important than realism.

No mechanic should ever remove player control unnecessarily.

Every animation should be cancellable where it improves skill expression.

---

## Core Pillars

1. Flow

Movement should chain together naturally.

Nothing should feel sticky.

Nothing should interrupt rhythm.

---

2. Expression

Players should look different because of skill.

Not because of skins.

Good players should naturally look impressive.

---

3. Practice

Players should always have somewhere to improve.

Movement Lab exists for this purpose.

---

4. Readability

Combat should always be readable.

Effects should never hide important information.

Every animation should communicate intention.

---

5. Performance

BLIKK must run smoothly on both potato PCs and high-end PCs.

Gameplay always takes priority over visual fidelity.

Every graphics option should scale appropriately.

---

## World

Players are professional fighters.

Movement Labs are official training facilities.

Lobbies are gathering places before matches.

Weapons and armour are training equipment developed for competitive combat.

Everything in BLIKK exists to support competitive movement.

---

## Art Direction

Dark.

Y2K.

Matrix.

Industrial.

Technical.

Underground.

Minimal.

Readable.

Never noisy.

---

## Audio Direction

Heavy impacts.

Mechanical reloads.

Clean sword sounds.

Subtle ambience.

Powerful movement sounds.

Every action should feel satisfying.

---

## Long Term Goal

Create the highest skill ceiling movement shooter ever built on Roblox.

Create mechanics that players spend thousands of hours mastering.

Build a game that players still talk about ten years later.
